# Ownership and open-source rules

- We control this repository, product branding, deployment, configurations and our original contributions. This does not give us exclusive rights to upstream code, dependencies or third-party APIs.
- The repository is public, but no project-wide open-source license has yet been selected. **Public is not automatically open-license.** The owner should choose a license and contribution policy with appropriate review before accepting substantial outside code.
- PNLCS is MIT-licensed and may be commercially adapted with required copyright/license notices. Other repositories may use GPL/AGPL with additional obligations. Do not copy their code into our original Django application without a license review.
- Prefer integration with well-maintained standalone services (Uptime Kuma, Umami, Certbot, etc.) when useful; treat each service as a separate deployment with its own license, updates and security responsibilities.
- Record third-party dependencies and notices. Do not use other companies' trademarks or copy protected designs.
- Ownership and licensing are legal matters; seek qualified advice before commercial release and contributor agreement decisions.

## Engineering effort and cost conventions

The ranges below are **rough one-engineer hands-on engineering-days** for already-scoped implementation, not elapsed dates, contractual quotes or guarantees. They exclude waiting for access, provider support, legal approval, security review and downstream rework. €/$/₹ operational prices are **deliberately not invented**. The largest actual cost risks are reseller wholesale registration/renewal funding, payment processor/settlement fees, email delivery, TLS/domain/hosting resources after credits expire, paid legal/accounting advice, DNS/VPS inventory, backups and monitoring. Never charge a card or registrar balance to validate a test.

## M5-08 first implementation: exact scope and acceptance

**Change type: ADAPT** existing code — `shop/views.py::nameservers` now reuses existing `shop/services.fulfillment_ready` and `_registrar_identity` rather than inventing a new adapter. On valid POST with local authenticated ownership, active status, verified saved customer handle, healthy compatible test/live configuration, matching registrar GET id/name/handle/ACT, send a single NS PUT and audit success. Any invalid/missing, timeout or mismatched state sends **zero** mutation requests. Unit fixtures in `shop/tests/domains/test_nameservers.py` cover these paths and cross-user 404.

**Non-goals:** no live provider calls, no DNS records, no transfer, no changes to database schema, no re-enable or switch change, no standalone management service. Registrar GET+PUT is not atomic with registrar transfers; operators must reconcile uncertain writes manually. Requiring full sales/fulfillment readiness also blocks existing customer NS management during sales maintenance; a dedicated separately approved management gate can be designed later.
